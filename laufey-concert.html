The Artist

Her Story

Laufey Lín Jónsdóttir — known to the world simply as Laufey — was born on June 23, 2001 in Reykjavík, Iceland. The daughter of an Icelandic father and a Chinese mother, she grew up between two worlds: the quiet drama of Nordic landscapes and the rich cultural tapestry of her Chinese heritage. From the earliest age, music was not a hobby but a language.

"I've always felt like music is the one place where I completely belong." — Laufey

Early Childhood · Reykjavík

A Prodigy in the Making

Laufey began studying cello at age five, quickly demonstrating extraordinary classical ability. Alongside her twin sister Steinunn, she grew up immersed in both Icelandic folk traditions and the sweeping world of classical music. The family home was always filled with Bach, Debussy, and the kind of quiet wonder that later defined her songwriting.

Teenage Years · Reykjavík Music Academy

Classical Roots, Jazz Wings

At the Reykjavík College of Music, Laufey immersed herself in classical cello performance while quietly falling in love with a very different tradition — the warm, late-night world of jazz. Artists like Chet Baker, Bill Evans, and Nat King Cole shaped the emotional vocabulary she would eventually pour into original songs. She taught herself guitar, piano, and the art of writing melodies that ached with longing.

2019 – 2022 · Berklee College of Music, Boston

Finding Her Voice

A scholarship brought her to Berklee, where the confluence of classical training and jazz theory gave her compositions their signature depth. She began posting original songs on social media, initially to a modest audience. Within months, her intimate, string-laced ballads found millions of listeners who recognized something rare: sincerity. Her debut EP Typical of Me (2021) announced an artist already fully formed.

2022

Everything I Know About Love

Her debut album arrived with the delicate force of a first snow. Spare arrangements of cello, upright bass, and brushed drums cradled lyrics that explored heartbreak, longing, and the bittersweet weight of growing up. Critics and fans alike responded to its unusual poise — here was a 21-year-old writing with the wisdom of someone who had lived many lives.

2023 – 2024

Bewitched — A Grammy Moment

Bewitched cemented Laufey as a generational talent. Lush, orchestral, and achingly romantic, the album earned her the Grammy Award for Best Traditional Pop Vocal Album — the youngest artist ever to claim the honour. An expanded Goddess Edition followed in 2024, featuring new orchestral arrangements and intimate acoustic recordings that stripped the songs back to their emotional core.

2025 – Present

A New Chapter

Rather than repeat a proven formula, Laufey disappeared into the studio with a small ensemble of trusted collaborators. She emerged with A Matter of Time: The Final Hour — something both familiar and startling, an album that pushes deeper into classical structure while pulsing with an urgency her earlier work never needed. Tomorrow night marks its live premiere.

2025 · New Release

The New Album

Recorded across studios in Reykjavík, London, and Los Angeles, A Matter of Time: The Final Hour is Laufey's most ambitious and most urgent work to date. Where Bewitched drifted in the timeless haze of new romance, this record is acutely aware of time — its passage, its cruelty, the way a single hour can hold an entire lifetime of feeling. Classical counterpoint weaves through jazz chord voicings; strings swell and recede like a clock no one can stop.

Laufey plays every stringed instrument on the record herself, and conducted the chamber ensemble that appears on seven of the ten tracks. It is a record about urgency — the things we almost said, the moments we let slip, the impossible weight of a final hour and what we choose to fill it with.
